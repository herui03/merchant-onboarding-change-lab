validation of the assumptions, business decisions on the open questions, and a real UAT signoff.

## What it does **not** support

- That the fictional business assumptions A-01…A-07 are true. They were authored, not validated.
- That the **submission-date** freshness rule (DEC-04) is acceptable to the business.
- That pausing decisions between the effective date and the migration run is operationally acceptable (R2).
- That grandfathered gaps such as MOB-0002 are an accepted risk (R3).
- Anything about real authentication, performance at scale, availability or data protection. None of these are in scope.

## Conditions before release

1. External UAT: business testers run AC-01…AC-25 (or their own scenarios) and sign the table on the **UAT evidence** page.
2. The policy owner decides open questions 1–3 in `03_assumptions_decisions.md` (freshness at decision date, migration timing, re-papering).
3. Replace the lab "Acting as" switch and simulated clock with real authentication and a real time source.
4. Legal/compliance confirm the real evidence rule. The 90-day window and V3/V4 bands here are fictional.

## Known limitations carried forward

DEC-04 (age as of submission date), DEC-06 (refused attempts are not persisted), DEC-12 (self-review covers
"created or submitted" only), POSIX-only run lock, Flask development server, synthetic data only.
